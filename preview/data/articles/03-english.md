Reading slowly is not a virtue. It is a way of letting a sentence finish before you answer it.

## I. The room

There is a room with one high window. In the morning the light comes in at an angle and stays on the wall for about an hour. Nobody built the room for that hour, but the hour is the reason the room is remembered.

## II. The page

A page is a small architecture. Margins hold the roof up; the line length decides how far you walk before turning. When the measure is too wide, the eye loses the thread. When it is too narrow, the sentence becomes a corridor.

## III. The light

The light does not ask to be useful. It arrives, it crosses the room, and it leaves. A good reading day is the same: nothing is solved, but something is seen.
